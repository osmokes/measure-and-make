**Measure and Make** is an independent engineering publication: long-form writing about how things are designed, built and measured, from bridges to build systems.

The site itself is built in the open, one milestone at a time, with [Astro](https://astro.build) and plain Markdown. This article is a sample used while the site is being built; it will be replaced before launch, that is coming soon!.
